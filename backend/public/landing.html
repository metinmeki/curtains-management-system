Each retail store keeps its own sales, clients, debts, expenses, and reports. Inventory has a separate wholesale workflow.

Data separation is the main rule of this system: retail store records do not mix with other stores or with inventory records.

Retail store modules

Dashboard, POS sales, debts, clients, expenses, discounts, payment status, and date-based filters.

Inventory modules

Warehouse dashboard, wholesale sales, clients, balances, debts, categories, product types, and reports.

Frontend scope

This prototype shows the user flow and screens while backend/database work remains separate.
